I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science (2024&ndash;Present) at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I am a member of the HKUST NLP Group led by Professor Junxian He, who is also my Ph.D. supervisor. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I completed my B.Eng. (2020&ndash;2024). Professor Junxian He also advised me during my undergraduate studies at SJTU.
